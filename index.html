About the project

A native recompilation built for modern PCs, connected to the original Dreamcast online experience.

Play Bomberman Online

Download the prebuilt native release for your platform, select your own Bomberman Online disc image, and play. The launcher supports Windows, macOS and Linux. Your game image is not included.
